MyFitCraft privacy policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026. MyFitCraft is a personal fitness-exercise app made by one individual developer (the developer).

The short version

  • Your exercises, sessions, photos and videos stay on your phone. They leave it only when you back them up to a folder you choose (for example in your own Google Drive), or when you choose to share an item.
  • The app has no ads and no tracking by advertisers. We do not sell your data.
  • You can use the app without an account. You can delete your account and the data held for it from inside the app at any time.

What the app sends to our server

The app uses an online service (Supabase) for the shared exercise library, optional accounts, feedback and the AI session builder.

  • An anonymous account. On first use your phone is signed in to an account with no name or email. It has a random id. This lets the app download the shared library and apply daily limits.
  • Optional sign-in. If you sign in with an email code, Google or Apple, your email address is attached to your account. We receive the email address and a sign-in token, not your Google or Apple password.
  • AI session builder. When you use it, the text of your request and a list of library exercises that might fit are sent to our server and on to Anthropic (the Claude AI) to build a session. We store only a count of requests per day, not the text. Anthropic processes the text under its own terms.
  • Feedback. If you send feedback, the message you wrote, the screen you were on, your phone type (Android or iPhone), the app version and your account id are stored. Spoken feedback is turned into text on your phone using the phone's own speech recognition (Apple or Google, depending on the phone). We receive and keep only the text, never audio.
  • Sharing. If you tap Share on an exercise or session, its text, photos and videos are uploaded and wait for review by the developer. If accepted, the item may become part of the shared library, which other people can download. It is not shown with your name.
  • Usage data (you can turn it off). The app records which screens and features are used, as counts, with the app version, phone type and a random id for each app launch. It never includes anything you type, exercise names, photos, or AI requests. The switch is on the first-launch screen and on the Back up screen.
  • Library membership. If a paid library is offered later, whether your account has it is stored with your account id.

What stays on your phone

Your own exercises, sessions, notes, photos and videos are stored on your phone. Backups go to a folder you choose, for example in your own Google Drive; we cannot see them.

The app asks for the camera and photos to add exercise pictures and videos, and the microphone to take spoken requests and feedback. These are used only when you use those features.

Who else handles data

  • Supabase stores the account, library, feedback, shares and usage data.
  • Anthropic processes AI session builder requests.
  • Google and Apple process sign-in if you choose it, and provide the app stores and speech recognition.

We do not sell personal data and do not share it for advertising.

How long we keep it

Account, feedback and usage data are kept until you delete your account, or until the developer removes them. Items you shared that were accepted into the shared library stay in the library as library content, without your account or any contact details.

Deleting your account and data

In the app: open the Account screen (the person icon at the top) and tap Delete my account. This deletes your account, your feedback, usage data, AI request counts, membership, and shares that were still waiting for review. Your exercises and sessions on the phone stay.

Without the app: email bluewindnesta@gmail.com from the address on your account (or with your account id, shown on the Account screen) and ask for deletion. We will delete it within 30 days.

You can also ask us what we hold about you, or to correct it, at the same address.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we update the date above, and the app asks again where the change matters.
